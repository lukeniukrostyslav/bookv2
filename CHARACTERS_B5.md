# B5 — CHARACTERS

## Elia Valli — protagonist

Age: 39.
Occupation: municipal sound archivist and restoration specialist.
Lives alone in a small apartment above a closed print shop near the old town.

### External goal
Determine how Mara Venturi's recording could exist and who sent it to him.

### Internal wound
Eleven years earlier, Elia's younger brother Matteo disappeared from Porto Nero after an argument. The official investigation concluded that Matteo left voluntarily. Elia never accepted that conclusion.

### Secret
Elia once found an audio fragment connected to Matteo but destroyed the original after his father convinced him that pursuing it would destroy the family. He has never told anyone.

### Strengths
Patient, observant, technically skilled, unusually sensitive to inconsistencies in sound.

### Weaknesses
Obsessive once he finds an anomaly; distrustful of institutions; tends to confuse evidence with certainty.

### Arc
He begins believing that enough evidence can reconstruct any truth. He must eventually confront the possibility that evidence can be manipulated and that his own memories are part of the investigation.

---

## Lucia Serra — police inspector

Age: 44.
Role: local police inspector.

### External goal
Prevent an old unresolved disappearance from becoming a public scandal while determining whether a new crime has occurred.

### Internal conflict
Lucia believes procedure protects people from false accusations. She fears acting on intuition because an earlier case in her career was damaged by an unsupported theory.

### Relationship to Elia
Professional respect mixed with irritation. She knows he is good at finding details but worries that he becomes emotionally attached to them.

### Secret
Lucia remembers a detail from the Matteo investigation that was never included in the official report.

### Arc
She moves from procedural distance toward acknowledging that institutional records can contain omissions without assuming conspiracy.

---

## Mara Venturi — recorded woman

Age at death: 33.
Occupation: acoustics technician who occasionally worked with municipal infrastructure projects.

### Function
The first impossible voice and emotional centre of the mystery.

### Character
Practical, dry humour, highly observant, uncomfortable with authority.

### Secret
Mara had been investigating unusual audio interference around the old customs warehouse shortly before her death.

### Canon limit
The precise cause of the recording anomaly remains UNKNOWN.

---

## Matteo Valli — Elia's missing brother

Age when disappeared: 24.
Occupation: apprentice marine electrician.

### Function
Personal connection between Elia and Porto Nero's forgotten history.

### Canon
Officially disappeared eleven years before the opening. His fate is UNKNOWN.

### Important rule
Matteo is not automatically the solution to the central mystery. His disappearance may intersect with it, but the story must earn the connection.

---

## Tomas Rinaldi — hotel engineer

Age: 52.
Works at Hotel Aurora, which stands on the former Venturi property.

### Function
Provides technical knowledge of the building and its hidden infrastructure.

### Character
Quiet, practical, dislikes rumours.

### Secret
He knows the hotel's underground service corridors preserve parts of the old foundation.

---

## Sofia Venturi — Mara's younger sister

Age: 37.
Works as a school music teacher in another city and returns to Porto Nero after Elia contacts her.

### Function
Human connection to Mara rather than an exposition device.

### Conflict
She wants the past left alone because she has spent ten years rebuilding her life after Mara's death.

### Secret
Mara sent Sofia a message three days before her death that Sofia never showed police.

---

## Character rules

1. No character exists only to explain the plot.
2. Secrets must create consequences.
3. Character knowledge must be tracked separately from reader knowledge.
4. No character is secretly the mastermind unless later evidence and motivation support it.
5. Matteo's fate remains UNKNOWN.
6. Mara's recording mechanism remains UNKNOWN.
