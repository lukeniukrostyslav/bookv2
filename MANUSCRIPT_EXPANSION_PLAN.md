Project: Book V2 — THE LAST RECORDING

Target commercial range: 80,000–95,000 words

## Purpose

The current manuscript has the complete 32-chapter + epilogue story architecture, but it is only 5,810 words. The next production stage is to expand the existing story into a full-length commercial novel without changing the established canon or reveal order.

This is an expansion of the existing manuscript, not a new story.

## Production rules

1. Preserve the established ending, central mystery, characters, chronology and fair-play logic.
2. Do not pad scenes with empty prose.
3. Expand through dramatized scenes, investigation steps, character interaction, sensory atmosphere, evidence handling, false hypotheses, consequences and emotional progression.
4. Keep the grounded technological explanation: no supernatural mechanism and no time travel.
5. Preserve the 31-second motif as a mechanical/thematic signal.
6. Preserve Mara's dead-drop architecture and Matteo's established fate.
7. Preserve Vieri's legally precise responsibility.
8. Every new scene must either deepen character, advance investigation, strengthen atmosphere, plant/pay off evidence, or increase consequence.
9. No new major mystery may be introduced unless it has a planned payoff.
10. Re-run continuity and fair-play audit only after the expanded manuscript is complete.

## Target distribution

Working target: approximately 85,000 words.

The 32 chapters will be expanded to roughly 2,400–3,000 words each, with flexibility based on scene needs. The epilogue remains comparatively short.

## Acceptance gate

Expansion is complete only when:
- canonical manuscript is within the agreed commercial target range;
- all 32 chapters + epilogue remain coherent;
- no canon contradiction is introduced;
- character arcs remain consistent;
- clues have fair-play placement;
- climax follows from established evidence;
- B12 continuity/clue/fair-play audit is re-run on the expanded manuscript.

## Progress rule

Do not count a chapter as complete merely because words were added. Each expansion phase requires scene-level quality verification.
