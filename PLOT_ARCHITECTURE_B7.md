# B7 — PLOT ARCHITECTURE

## Structural target
Three-act psychological mystery/thriller. Target length 80,000–95,000 words; 32 chapters plus short epilogue. Close third person, primarily Elia. Plot movement: question → evidence → interpretation → contradiction → new question.

## Act I — THE IMPOSSIBLE VOICE (Ch. 1–8)
1. 08:17 — Elia receives Mara's cassette and confirms the impossible timing.
2. The Missing Minute — restoration reveals hidden audio: “Room twelve.”
3. The Second File — anonymous sender supplies Mara's second audio file.
4. Official Time — Lucia confirms death time; Elia finds independent archive timestamps.
5. Room Twelve — old numbered service space beneath customs infrastructure is identified.
6. Sofia's Silence — Sofia gives Elia Mara's withheld final message.
7. Matteo — Lucia reveals the omitted audio-device detail from Matteo's case.
8. The First False Theory — evidence suggests Mara survived; Elia disproves the theory. Act I turn: someone is deliberately feeding him evidence.

## Act II-A — THE PATTERN (Ch. 9–16)
9. Three Voices — older disappearance recordings share a background signature.
10. 31 Seconds — recurring interval becomes the first mechanical signature.
11. The Old System — Tomas explains remnants of customs acoustic infrastructure.
12. The Wrong Timestamp — Elia proves a historical recording was archived later than its capture.
13. The Listener — someone accesses archive metadata after hours.
14. Lucia's Omission — Matteo's recovered device contained a voice fragment linked to the customs warehouse.
15. The Second Recording — Mara's file gives a location sequence leading to Hotel Aurora's underground corridors.
16. The Empty Room — Room 12 contains obsolete equipment and evidence of recent use. Midpoint: impossible recordings are being manufactured.

## Act II-B — THE MANUFACTURED TRUTH (Ch. 17–24)
17. The Copy — Elia finds evidence that another copy of his original Matteo fragment existed.
18. The Archive Is Compromised — a catalog record is altered while Elia works.
19. Vieri — Alessandro Vieri appears publicly as a respected heritage official.
20. The Preservation Project — Vieri's organization has legitimate access to infrastructure records.
21. The Sound Under the Sound — a hidden channel contains an instruction.
22. Matteo's Last Route — evidence reconstructs his final movements.
23. The Trap — a recording apparently proving Matteo is alive is exposed as manufactured.
24. The Missing Minute — Mara's final minute is recovered and points to a physical recording route. Act II turn: Mara knew the system could be used against Elia and built a test into the recording.

## Act III — THE LAST RECORDING (Ch. 25–32)
25. The Route — Elia maps the acoustic path from archive to customs infrastructure.
26. What Vieri Knows — Vieri indirectly demonstrates knowledge that implies monitoring.
27. The Original — the original source proves the timestamp was manufactured.
28. Matteo — truth revealed: Matteo discovered the network and died trying to preserve evidence; his disappearance was disguised through manipulated records.
29. The Ledger — evidence connects the network to multiple disappearances and Vieri's role.
30. The Last Minute — Mara explains why she chose Elia and warns him about the operator.
31. The Broadcast — Vieri attempts to destroy physical evidence; Elia uses the network to preserve an unaltered master and send evidence to Lucia.
32. After the Noise — investigation begins; Elia accepts that truth cannot restore the lost years.

## Epilogue — 31 Seconds
Months later, an ordinary restoration contains the same 31-second interval. Elia finds no hidden message and closes the file. This is not supernatural; it shows that evidence can outlive manipulation.

## Reveal ladder
1. Impossible timestamp. 2. Hidden audio. 3. Room Twelve. 4. Matteo connection. 5. 31-second signature. 6. Timestamp manipulation. 7. Hidden network. 8. Manufactured recordings. 9. Vieri's access. 10. Original source. 11. Matteo's fate. 12. Mara's missing minute. 13. Full mechanism and motive.

## Fair-play rules
- Every major twist has an earlier observable clue.
- No new technology appears only at the reveal.
- Physical locations precede explanations of their function.
- Vieri appears before his antagonist role is known.
- Matteo's fate is established through evidence, not a convenient confession.
- The central mechanism is solvable shortly before Elia solves it.
- Red herrings are disproved by evidence.
- No chapter reveals more than two major mystery layers.

## Emotional arc
Elia begins believing evidence can recover everything. He learns evidence can be manipulated. His final victory is distinguishing recording from event, evidence from interpretation, and truth from the story imposed on evidence.
