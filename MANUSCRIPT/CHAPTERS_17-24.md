# MANUSCRIPT — CHAPTERS 17-24

## Chapter 17 — The Copy

The paper marked M.V. was not a note.

It was an inventory code.

Elia photographed it before touching anything.

Back at the archive, he searched the old accession records.

M.V.

Nothing.

Then he searched the initials separately.

Mara Venturi.

Matteo Valli.

A storage transfer appeared.

Eleven years earlier.

One item had been transferred from police custody to the municipal archive.

Description:

**digital audio copy — unidentified source.**

Status:

**withdrawn.**

Elia stared at the word.

"Who withdrew it?"

Lucia leaned over the screen.

"That's impossible."

"Why?"

"Because I did."

She went pale.

"I never sent it here."

Elia opened the transfer history.

A second copy had been created.

Then deleted.

Then restored.

The timestamps formed a perfect sequence.

Too perfect.

Someone had been rehearsing the past.

## Chapter 18 — The Archive Is Compromised

The catalogue changed while Elia was looking at it.

A line disappeared.

Then returned.

Then changed again.

Lucia watched the screen.

"Don't touch anything."

"I'm not."

A new authorization appeared.

**Vieri Heritage Foundation.**

Lucia took a photograph.

"Do you know what that means?"

"Access."

"No."

She looked at him.

"It means somebody with legitimate credentials is inside our system."

Elia checked the archive server.

The login was not local.

It came through the maintenance network.

The same network connected to Room 12.

A printer started by itself.

One sheet emerged.

A single sentence:

**STOP RESTORING WHAT WAS BURIED.**

Lucia picked it up.

For the first time, Elia saw fear in her face.

Not fear of a person.

Fear of what the person might be able to erase.

## Chapter 19 — Vieri

Alessandro Vieri arrived at the archive the following afternoon.

He wore a dark coat and carried no umbrella.

"Signor Valli."

"You know me."

"I know your work."

Vieri smiled.

"I've heard you're very good with damaged things."

Elia did not invite him inside.

Vieri looked past him.

"The waterfront restoration has uncovered several old infrastructure records. I wanted to make sure the archive was not overwhelmed."

"That's kind."

"It isn't kindness."

Vieri smiled again.

"It's administration."

He knew about Mara.

He knew about the customs plans.

He knew Elia had requested the Room 12 documents.

He should not have known any of it.

Elia said:

"How did you know about Room Twelve?"

Vieri paused.

"Old buildings have long memories."

Then he looked directly at Elia.

"Your brother learned that."

Silence.

Vieri left.

Lucia waited until the door closed.

"That was not a warning."

"What was it?"

"Proof."

## Chapter 20 — The Preservation Project

Vieri's foundation had funded restoration work around the harbour.

The official purpose was preservation.

The contractors' logs told another story.

They had repaired cables beneath Hotel Aurora.

They had accessed Room 12.

They had replaced old locks.

And they had installed new power.

All legally.

All documented.

Except for one missing contractor record.

Elia found it in a backup.

**Project technician: A. Rinaldi.**

Tomas stared at the screen.

"My brother."

"You never told me he worked there."

"He didn't."

The signature was wrong.

The handwriting was not Tomas's brother's.

Someone had used his name.

Lucia said:

"That's how they operate."

"Through legitimate records."

"Through records that look legitimate."

Elia understood.

The system did not hide the truth.

It buried it under paperwork.

## Chapter 21 — The Sound Under the Sound

Mara's second recording contained another layer.

Elia isolated it slowly.

The voice was weak.

But clear.

"If you are hearing this, don't trust the date."

A pause.

"Trust the room."

Another pause.

"The original has no audience."

Elia looked at Lucia.

"What does that mean?"

"I don't know."

The voice continued.

"If they give you a story, find the sound that couldn't belong to it."

Then came three clicks.

Mara's final words were almost inaudible.

"Matteo understood."

The file ended.

Elia replayed it.

Again.

Again.

The phrase stayed the same.

**Trust the room.**

Not the date.

The room.

Elia looked at the map.

Room 12 was not a recording room.

It was a relay point.

The recordings were entering the network there.

But where did they begin?

## Chapter 22 — Matteo's Last Route

The ferry timetable from eleven years ago gave them the first anchor.

Matteo had left the harbour at 21:17.

A witness remembered seeing him near the freight line.

Another heard the customs gate close.

A third remembered a motorcycle.

The official report said Matteo disappeared between 21:00 and midnight.

Elia rebuilt the route.

Harbour.

Freight line.

Customs warehouse.

Room 12.

Then another location.

An old electrical substation.

Matteo had gone there deliberately.

Inside the archive's old plans, Elia found a handwritten note.

Not Mara's.

Matteo's.

**If they can hear this, they can change the story.**

Below it:

**Do not let Elia destroy the original.**

Elia stared at the sentence.

He had destroyed an audio fragment eleven years ago.

Or thought he had.

Someone had known.

## Chapter 23 — The Trap

The file arrived at 23:11.

No message.

Just a name:

MATTEO_FINAL.

Elia almost deleted it.

Then he heard the first word.

"Elia."

His brother's voice.

Older.

Tired.

Alive.

Elia stopped breathing.

Matteo spoke for forty-seven seconds.

He described the harbour.

The warehouse.

Room 12.

Then:

"I should have told you."

Elia's eyes filled.

Lucia said:

"Wait."

She pointed at the waveform.

There was no room tone.

None.

A voice without a room.

Elia replayed it.

The harbour horn was there.

The gate.

The ferry engine.

But they overlapped unnaturally.

Three different recordings.

Three different distances.

One voice.

It was a composite.

A manufactured memory.

Elia closed the file.

The cruelty of it was worse than the lie.

Someone knew exactly what he wanted to believe.

## Chapter 24 — The Missing Minute

The final minute of Mara's first recording had not been erased.

It had been physically separated from the tape.

Elia found it inside the envelope's cardboard lining.

A narrow strip.

He restored it carefully.

Mara's voice returned.

"If you're hearing this, they have already shown you something false."

A breath.

"They will use Matteo."

Elia closed his eyes.

"I am sorry."

The recording continued.

"The date is a door. The room is the key."

A mechanical click.

Then Mara described a sequence of sounds.

Ferry.

Gate.

Relay.

Fan.

She was not giving a location.

She was giving a route.

"If you follow it, you will find where the recording was made."

A pause.

"But don't follow the recording."

Another pause.

"Follow the original."

The last seconds contained one final sound.

A man entering a room.

A key turning.

Then a voice.

Not clear enough for a name.

But Elia recognized the breathing.

He had heard it yesterday.

Vieri.

The tape stopped.

Lucia looked at him.

"Now we know."

Elia shook his head.

"No."

He looked toward the harbour.

"Now we know where to look."
