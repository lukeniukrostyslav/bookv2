## Strategic conclusion

The project should NOT attempt to copy a currently successful title.

The strongest working territory for Book V2 is:
**high-concept psychological mystery/thriller + strong atmosphere + emotional character stakes + an original central mystery.**

This gives us:
- immediate reader-facing tension;
- room for a distinctive premise;
- room for twists without relying on twists alone;
- potential for a standalone novel;
- potential for a later series if the story naturally supports it.

## Commercial principles

1. Hook must be understandable in one or two sentences.
2. First chapter must create a concrete unanswered question.
3. Mystery must have a fair internal logic.
4. Twists must be prepared by earlier information.
5. Characters need personal stakes independent of the puzzle.
6. Atmosphere should be distinctive enough to make the book recognizable.
7. Avoid trend-copying, derivative titles and familiar plot skeletons.
8. Commercial positioning is a target, not a guarantee of bestseller status.
